Hi, I'm Cem. Computer engineering by training, consulting by trade.

Ten years at PwC and Axon Partners, across Türkiye, Europe and the Gulf. Forensics and investigations early on, then regulatory and transformation work in the Gulf. I work in English, French and Turkish.

What I like is work that sits between the technical and the commercial. You need enough of both to see the whole picture. And I always end up at the same question: where is the value here, and who is paying for it?

Outside that I produce electronic music, mostly on hardware. I play strategy games, the long ones. And I'm passionate about cars and car culture.
